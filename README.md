## KYC: three levels, and which one you need

Gate's verification runs in stages, and the names have shifted over the years, so here's the practical breakdown.

| Level | What it asks for | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic (KYC1) | Name, nationality, residence, document number | Basic account access and identity records |
| Verification Plus (KYC2) | Photo of your ID plus a live face scan | Deposits, trading, withdrawals, new-user rewards |
| Proof of address (KYC3) | Address details, sometimes a supporting document | Higher deposit and withdrawal ceilings |

KYC2 is the one that matters. Without it, you can look at the interface, but you can't move money. With it, the standard limits apply and you become eligible for the Rewards Hub tasks.

The face scan is where people get stuck most often, and it's almost always an environment problem rather than a data problem. Good light, no hat, no sunglasses, plain background. Gate gives you two routes: scan with your computer camera, or scan a QR code and finish on your phone. If your browser refuses to cooperate — Chrome, Opera, Firefox, and Brave handle it best — the phone route takes about a minute. There's also a gesture-photo fallback that asks you to photograph yourself holding up the last digits of your ID number.

Reported processing time is short: third-party walkthroughs describe identity and address checks completing in minutes, and TradingFinder's step-by-step put the whole KYC flow at under ten minutes with documents ready. In practice, a clear photo passes automatically and a blurry one gets bounced back with a reason attached, which you can fix and resubmit.

Address verification (KYC3) is optional. Do it if you plan to move large amounts, because it's the lever that raises your withdrawal ceiling. Skip it if you're testing the platform with a small balance.

## Deposits, withdrawals, and what the first trade costs

Crypto deposits are free at Gate's end; you only cover the network fee charged by the chain you send from. Fiat gets in through card payments or third-party processors, or through peer-to-peer trading, and those channels set their own spreads and fees that are shown before you confirm.

Withdrawals are where the cost is less predictable. Gate recalculates withdrawal fees roughly hourly based on network congestion, and the fee plus minimum appears on screen once you've picked a coin and a network. Sending USDT over a high-traffic network costs noticeably more than a cheaper one. If you're withdrawing small amounts, consolidate them rather than paying fees on each transfer.

Your fee history is visible in two places: Assets → Billing Details → Unified Account for the currency-level breakdown, and Spot → Trade History for per-trade fees. Both are worth a look after your first trade, because if you've enabled GT deduction the number on screen won't match the flat VIP rate.

## Individual accounts vs institutional

Gate separates two account paths, and they don't overlap as much as people assume.

- **Individual (VIP).** One person, one account, tiered fees, borrowing discounts at higher tiers, VIP-only wealth products, and a dedicated account manager at the top end. This is what a normal registration produces.
- **Institutional.** Built for teams running multiple strategies: OTC settlement, custody, API connectivity, custom fee structures, and market-maker arrangements. You don't get these by reaching a VIP level — they require a separate application and evaluation.

If you're a solo trader who simply trades a lot, the VIP path is the one to optimize. If you're registering on behalf of a fund or a business, say so early, because the onboarding route is different.

## Common problems and quick fixes

**"The referral code field is missing."** It's collapsed under the email/phone input. Click the small arrow beside it. If you've already registered, it's too late — that field locks.

**"My KYC keeps failing."** Nine times out of ten it's image quality or glare. Retake the ID photos on a dark, flat surface, make sure all four corners are visible, and don't crop. If the face scan loops, switch from the desktop camera to the phone app via QR code.

**"I can't deposit."** Unverified accounts can't move funds. Complete KYC2 first.

**"Why is my withdrawal limited?"** You're on the standard limit for your tier. Completing proof-of-address verification raises it, and so does climbing the VIP ladder.

**"I registered but see no rewards."** New-user tasks must be claimed manually from the Rewards Hub, and they expire 15 days after registration. Advanced challenge rewards are distributed automatically after the campaign settles.
